Email / Google Scholar / GitHub / CV

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working with Prof. Songan Zhang. I received my Ph.D. degree in Aeronautics and Astronautics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Jinfang Teng at the United Innovation Center (UIC) of Aerothermal Technologies for Turbomachinery.

My research interest lies in turbomachinery aerodynamics, next-generation advanced propulsion, and AI for Science. Currently, my research focuses on leveraging AI to advance and elevate aerodynamic design capabilities for advanced turbomachinery.
